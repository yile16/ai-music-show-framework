# Direct Codex Version: AI Universe Music Show

## One-Sentence Core

Create a repeatable AI production workflow that turns public-domain or original characters into short-video music MV episodes, starting with one character and eventually expanding into a universe-scale AI music variety show.

## What This Really Is

This is not just an AI skill. It is three things layered together:

1. A creative IP engine: characters, worldview, host/system, narrative incentives.
2. A production pipeline: research, song, visual identity, storyboard, image, video, review, publish.
3. An agent orchestration framework: deciding what AI agents do, what existing tools do, where humans approve, and what later becomes scalable automation.

## MVP Boundary

The first milestone should be a single-character text-first demo package.

Do not start by building a platform. Do not start by producing ten videos. Start by producing one complete package for one character, probably Sun Wukong or another copyright-safe figure.

The first package should include:

- Character research packet
- Dramatic biography
- Spirit/personality/music extraction
- Universe premise for why the character sings
- Song brief and lyrics prompt
- Visual bible
- Supporting cast and stage setting
- Storyboard and shot prompts
- Tool candidates
- Review gates
- Production checklist

## First Agent Candidates

Build or configure these first:

1. Character Research Agent: collects sources, separates fact/tradition/invention, flags uncertainty.
2. Character Spirit Agent: extracts conflict, values, emotional palette, music direction, and audience hook.
3. Song Package Agent: creates Suno-ready song brief, lyrics structure, hook lines, and revision notes.
4. Visual Bible Agent: produces consistent, copyright-aware character design prompts.
5. Storyboard Agent: turns song and visual bible into shot-level MV prompts.
6. Tool Discovery Agent: searches existing tools/skills before you build new ones.
7. Review Gate Agent: checks completeness, copyright risk, platform risk, and whether the work is ready for generation.

## What Needs Scale Later

- Batch character production
- Prompt/version management
- Asset consistency tracking
- Image-to-video batch generation
- Video editing and shot assembly
- Publishing metadata
- Analytics feedback loop
- Reusing successful variety-show formats and viral scene templates

## Human Review Should Stay Here

- Character selection
- Final creative canon
- Song taste
- Visual identity approval
- Legal/copyright judgment
- Final publishing decision

## Suggested First Demo Flow

1. Pick Sun Wukong.
2. Define episode premise: a universe show invites him to perform because audience attention converts into divine power.
3. Research and summarize biography.
4. Extract spirit: rebellion, freedom, impossible pride, playful violence, loneliness after victory.
5. Choose music direction: high-energy Chinese myth rock / cyber opera / battle anthem.
6. Generate Suno-compatible song brief and lyrics.
7. Create original visual bible, avoiding modern protected depictions.
8. Draft 8-12 shot vertical MV storyboard.
9. Generate one reference image.
10. Generate one music track.
11. Generate 2-3 video shots.
12. Review whether the concept is worth scaling.

## The Most Important Strategic Choice

Your first deliverable should be a reusable character package template, not a polished video. Once the package is strong, media generation becomes an execution problem. If the package is weak, every image and video step will amplify the weakness.
